Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

01. Information Collection

We collect essential data to facilitate logistics, including name, physical address for pickup/delivery, contact numbers, and order history.

02. Data Usage

Data is strictly used for order fulfillment, capacity optimization, and critical service notifications. We do not sell user data to third-party advertisers.

03. Security Layers

Sensitive payment information is handled via Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant). Our internal systems use multi-layer encryption for storage.

04. Retention

Account data is retained for the duration of your active subscription and 24 months post-deactivation to comply with local financial reporting standards.
